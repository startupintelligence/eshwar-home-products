Frequently Asked
Questions

Answers to common questions about our traditional pantry range, store opening hours, and WhatsApp order enquiries.

What products do you carry at Eshwar Home Products?

We stock traditional South Indian rice varieties, Siruthaniyam whole millets and millet flours (Thinai, Varagu, Samai, Kuthiraivali, Ragi, Kambu), wood-pressed Chekku oils, clarified cow ghee, wild honey, pulses, dry fruits, and millet snacks.

How can I place an order or check today's stock?

Send your pantry list on WhatsApp or call +91 99412 09953. Because harvest varieties and pack sizes vary day to day, our store team confirms current availability, pricing, and pickup directly with you.

Do you have online checkout or home delivery?

Our website serves as an exploratory product guide without automated online checkout. Please message us on WhatsApp or call during store hours to arrange your order and confirm pickup or local options.

What are your store opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM (IST), and closed on Sundays. Please call ahead on public holidays to verify hours.

How can I check my order status or track pickup and delivery?

Since all pantry orders are coordinated personally through WhatsApp or phone rather than an automated checkout portal, you can check your order status anytime by replying to your existing WhatsApp thread or calling +91 99412 09953 with your name and item list during store hours (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM). Our counter team will let you know right away if your packs are packed, ready for pickup, or out for local dispatch.

Where is the Perambur store located?

Find us at 119/1, Paddy Field Road, Chinnaiyan Colony, Perambur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600011, India.
